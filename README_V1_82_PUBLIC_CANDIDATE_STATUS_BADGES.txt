Candidate Registration Portal V1.82

Built cumulatively on V1.81 and the attached working V1.77 base.

- Every candidate shown in the public applicant gallery now has a status badge.
- Approved is displayed in green.
- Pending is displayed in orange.
- Rejected is displayed in red.
- Not-submitted or incomplete applications are presented publicly as Pending,
  ensuring every displayed applicant has one of the three requested statuses.
- Presidential applicants remain excluded from the public gallery.
