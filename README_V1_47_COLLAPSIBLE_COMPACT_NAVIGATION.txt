V1.47 - COLLAPSIBLE COMPACT MAIN NAVIGATION

The Main Navigation Dashboard is now organized into three collapsible sections:

- Registration & Lookup
- Polling Terminals
- Results Dashboards

Only one section remains open at a time. Registration & Lookup is open on first
load, while the other sections stay collapsed to reduce scrolling and visual
scatter. The header, cards, spacing, typography and service descriptions have
also been condensed for desktop and mobile screens.
