Candidate Registration Portal V1.79

Built cumulatively on V1.78, which was built directly on the attached working
V1.77 Application/Amendment Date package.

- Public applicant gallery now includes every ODM and UDA applicant who has an
  uploaded passport photograph.
- Pending, approved, rejected, not-submitted/incomplete and administratively
  inactive records are no longer excluded from the public gallery.
- Party, county, constituency, ward and position filters remain unchanged.
- Private applicant data and uploaded documents remain inaccessible publicly.
